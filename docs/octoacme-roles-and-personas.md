# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Manager / Delivery Lead

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers or Delivery Leads are responsible for the sustained execution of delivery teams. They help turn planned work into predictable team output while balancing capacity, quality, and technical risk.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate sprint or iteration execution with the PM and Product Lead
- Manage team capacity, workload distribution, and dependencies
- Identify delivery risks and escalate blockers early
- Support engineering practices, planning quality, and team health
- Partner with developers and stakeholders to ensure realistic commitments

### Goals
- Maintain predictable delivery and healthy team performance
- Reduce unnecessary context switching and execution drag
- Improve alignment between technical execution and business priorities

### Typical Communication
- Weekly delivery reviews and backlog refinement sessions
- Team planning and dependency management discussions
- Escalation updates with PMs and leadership when risk or capacity issues arise

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers on schedule and reporting
- Aligns with Product Managers on scope trade-offs and priority changes
- Supports Developers by removing blockers and clarifying technical constraints
- Coordinates with QA and Security leads to protect release readiness

---

## Security / Compliance Lead

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Leads ensure that project work meets required security, privacy, and policy standards. They help embed risk management into project work rather than treating it as a final-stage gate alone.

### Responsibilities
- Review project requirements for security, privacy, and compliance impact
- Identify policy, data handling, and access control considerations
- Partner with engineering on remediation plans and secure design decisions
- Support release readiness by validating that risks are reduced to acceptable levels
- Help communicate escalation paths for incidents or critical vulnerabilities

### Goals
- Reduce risk exposure without slowing delivery unnecessarily
- Ensure compliance requirements are addressed early and consistently
- Improve trust in project outcomes and production readiness

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews and architecture check-ins
- Risk and compliance updates during project and release meetings
- Incident or vulnerability escalation communications

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Shares risk and control requirements with Product and Project leads
- Advises Developers on secure implementation patterns and remediation priorities
- Works with QA to confirm verification of security-related acceptance criteria
- Escalates critical concerns to project sponsors or leadership when needed

---

## QA Lead / Test Manager

### Role Summary
QA Leads define the quality bar for a project and help ensure that work meets functional, operational, and user acceptance standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality strategy, test approach, and release readiness criteria
- Own test planning for unit, integration, regression, and end-to-end validation
- Coordinate test execution and defect triage with engineering stakeholders
- Validate acceptance criteria and user-facing readiness before release
- Support continuous improvement of testing practices and automation coverage

### Goals
- Catch defects earlier and reduce production risk
- Build confidence in feature readiness and release quality
- Increase test coverage and quality consistency across teams

### Typical Communication
- Test planning and readiness reviews
- Defect triage and release quality discussions
- Status updates on quality risks and validation progress

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to validate that acceptance criteria are testable
- Partners with Developers to verify fixes and regression risk
- Coordinates with PMs and Delivery Leads on release sequencing and quality gates
- Escalates unresolved quality risks upstream to leadership when needed

---

## Customer Success / Enablement Lead

### Role Summary
Customer Success or Enablement Leads connect delivery work to customer impact, adoption, and support readiness. They help ensure that what is delivered is usable, understandable, and ready for the people it is meant to serve.

### Responsibilities
- Translate customer feedback, support issues, and adoption signals into project input
- Help prioritize improvements that matter most to end users or internal teams
- Coordinate rollout readiness, training, and communications with stakeholders
- Identify support gaps, adoption barriers, or service readiness issues
- Partner with Product and PM functions on launch planning and feedback loops

### Goals
- Improve customer value and adoption of delivered solutions
- Reduce friction during launch or rollout
- Strengthen the feedback loop between delivery teams and users

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback reviews and stakeholder updates
- Launch readiness and enablement planning sessions
- Support escalation coordination and post-release feedback loops

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Feeds user insights and customer needs into Product prioritization
- Supports Project Managers with launch communications and stakeholder readiness
- Works with Developers and QA on usability and release quality considerations
- Helps identify implementation gaps that might affect adoption or supportability

---

## Data / Analytics Lead

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Leads help teams measure real-world impact and use evidence to guide delivery decisions. They support meaningful success metrics and operational visibility across the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Define success metrics, dashboards, and measurement plans for the initiative
- Partner with Product and PM leads to align metrics with business outcomes
- Monitor adoption, performance, and quality signals after launch
- Help interpret data to guide iteration, prioritization, and risk management
- Validate whether a release is delivering expected customer or operational value

### Goals
- Make decisions based on evidence rather than assumptions
- Improve visibility into product impact and operational health
- Support measurable continuous improvement after delivery

### Typical Communication
- Metric review sessions and reporting updates
- Performance or adoption reviews during milestone checkpoints
- Business impact summaries for stakeholders and leadership

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to refine success metrics and goals
- Shares operational signals with PMs and Delivery Leads to guide planning
- Supports QA and Security teams with release validation and monitoring data
- Helps Customer Success teams assess adoption and customer impact after launch

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Together, these roles clarify ownership, accountability, and collaboration across project planning, execution, quality, security, launch, and continuous improvement.
